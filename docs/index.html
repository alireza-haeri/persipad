A journal for Persian and English

PersiPad is an offline-first journal with a Persian (Solar Hijri) calendar. Write an entry, attach photos, track a daily score or set a recurring reminder. You can keep entries on your device without connecting a Google account.

Why connect Google Drive?

Connect your account in Settings → Data backup if you want to back up or restore your journal. PersiPad asks for basic profile information to identify the account and permission to manage its own hidden app data in Drive, not your general Drive files. Read the privacy policy before connecting.

About the project

Alireza Haeri maintains PersiPad as an independent fork of StoryPad. Credit for the original journal and editor goes to the StoryPad authors and contributors. Explore the source code or report an issue. Please do not post private entries or credentials in public issues.
