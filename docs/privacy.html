Privacy policy

This policy describes how PersiPad, maintained by Alireza Haeri, handles your journal and the Google account you may choose to connect.

On your device

Entries, drafts, attachment files, tags, reminders and settings are stored locally. You can write without signing in. When you export or share a file, the chosen destination receives that copy. A JSON export includes attachment metadata; the media archive contains the actual files.

Google account and backup data

Sign-in is optional. The openid, email and profile permissions let PersiPad identify your account and display information such as your name, email and profile picture. OAuth tokens are kept on your device so the app can access that account without asking for your Google password.

The drive.appdata permission lets PersiPad read, create, update and delete its own files in your Google Drive app data folder. It is used for backup and restore, not for browsing your personal Drive files. Backups can contain entries, settings and media you choose to sync. Google processes and stores those files to provide Drive. App-lock PINs and recovery answers are not included in the journal backup.

Services and diagnostics

Depending on platform and build configuration, Firebase services can process app usage events, device and app information, and crash diagnostics. Google account access is for the account and backup features described above, not for selling Google user data or serving personalized ads. See Google’s privacy policy for Google services.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can disconnect Drive in the app or revoke PersiPad’s access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access; it does not delete an existing backup. To remove hidden backup data, use Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → PersiPad → Options → Delete hidden app data, if available. Disconnect other syncing devices to avoid uploading a new backup.

To remove the local journal, clear the app’s storage on your device. Remove exported files separately from wherever you saved or shared them. These actions can be irreversible. For privacy or deletion questions, contact the maintainer below; never email passwords, tokens or private journal entries.
